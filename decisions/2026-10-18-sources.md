# Sources: a link for everything Bezalel quotes

2026-10-18. Status: accepted (Kevin's decisions below). Research:
the orchestrator's `sources-research.md` (inventory of every source, the
deep-link templates checked that day, the AI-translation findings).

## Context

The study tools returned Markdown only, each with its own credit style and
some with none; the model was told to label scripture and say where claims
come from, but not to link anything, and the only link after a quotation
was "(NET)" to netbible.org's front page. The credits page lists every
source's licence, but a reader of one answer couldn't open the verse, entry
or manuscript it rests on, and couldn't tell English the model translated
itself from a published translation.

## Kevin's decisions

- **Show sources for everything quoted**: a link to an online resource per
  record where there is one (verse, entry, note, passage, manuscript),
  otherwise a book citation (author, title, chapter or page).
- **The "(NET)" link goes to the verse** (`https://netbible.org/bible/{Book+Name}+{c}:{v}`):
  a page on netbible.org satisfies the NET's terms ("NET" linked to
  netbible.org).
- **The Sources list shows everything the tools consulted** for a reply,
  marking the ones the reply itself links ("cited").
- **Books** (Stott, Lennox, Rillera, Fee & Stuart, the Bradley paper) are
  cited by author, title and year; no edition, since the data records none.
- **English the model makes itself** from an original (Dead Sea Scrolls,
  Philo sections without English, Septuagint rows without Brenton, Onkelos)
  is labelled an AI translation, with a link to the original.
- **The HLT stays, relabelled.** The 26 "Heiser Literal Translation (HLT)"
  rows are shown as "Unpublished draft rendering (AI-assisted), not
  Heiser's", never attributed to Heiser, each with links to the original
  (the Hebrew on STEP THOT, the Greek LXX on STEP, and any Dead Sea Scroll
  witness's Leon Levy page). Heiser's own published arguments keep their
  links.
- The credits page stays the licence page.

## What was built

- `Bezalel.Study.Citation` (work, label, url, locator, book, licence,
  dataset_url, translation `:published | :assistant | :unpublished_draft`,
  original, cited) and `Bezalel.Study.Citations`, the one module that
  builds source URLs (templates: docs/study_tools.md#sources).
- Every study tool returns `{:details, markdown, %{citations: …}}` and
  writes one `Source:` line under each record, from the same citations.
  The ancient tools and `compare_biblical_versions` keep their licence
  lines. `get_textual_variant` prints the HLT rows as above, its result's
  `translation` field relabelled too, and the rationales' "HLT" read "the
  draft".
- `Bezalel.Agent.Details.keep_details/2` keeps `%{"card", "citations"}` in the
  session (replacing the apps' `Card.from_tool` one-liners);
  `Bezalel.Agent.Reply` collects citations (`Reply.sources/1` marks the
  cited), `Bezalel.Library.Message.citations` holds them for a stored
  reply, and sessions from before (the bare card as details) still read.
- Cited means the reply contains the citation's whole URL (or, for a
  book, its title). Prompt rule 5 now links "(NET)" to the verse; rule 6:
  link what you use, only links a tool gave, books as the tool gives
  them, and your own translations said to be yours, with the original.
- The passage card's `version_url` is the first quoted NET verse's page,
  and each verse has its `url`.
- Abbott-Smith page links: `priv/study/abbott_smith_pages.json`, built
  by data/'s `mix bezalel.data.abbott_smith` from the pinned TEI's page
  breaks and the Internet Archive scan's page numbers. No pack changed;
  every other link is computed from what the packs already store
  (Aquifer's dictionary and key-term files are one per initial, past a
  leading "The", checked against all 6,722 titles of both repos at their
  pins; Leon Levy pages for biblical manuscripts are exactly those the
  scrolls pack names).

## Verified

On 2026-10-18, beyond the research's checks: NET verse pages and Bible
Hub verse pages for all 66 books' 1:1; eBible Brenton's file names
(`PSA001`, `DAG01`, `EZR`/`NEH`); STEP's `@` separator for THOT, THGNT,
LXX and TNotes; Theographic event slugs (an apostrophe or comma becomes
`_`: `nimrod_s_kingdom_begins_87`, `death_of_eli_ark_captured_152`) and
the trailing slash its pages redirect to; ACAI file names by id
(`Cistern of sirah.md`, `Ability.2.md`); Cambridge's CD MS B
(`MS-TS-00016-00311`); the Israel Museum's war and temple pages; chaver.com
unit pages (without `.html`). Every URL the tools produced in a run over
the real packs (111 distinct) answered 200, but opentn.bible (Cloudflare).

## Not verified / caveats

- The Internet Archive viewer's `n{index}` page token for the two pages the
  scan didn't number (1, 2) assumes a zero-based leaf index `[INFERENCE]`.
- STEP links are search URLs, stable in practice but not documented
  permalinks. Leon Levy pages are a JavaScript app (200 for any siglum),
  hence the scrolls pack's names as the list of real pages.
- Book years are first-publication years from bibliographic knowledge
  `[INFERENCE]`, not read from studybible-mcp's data (it records titles
  only).
- The textual-variants data has no `source_url` for witnesses or author
  claims in data version 1, so those print no Source line; Heiser's
  published papers are linked from `get_theology_context`.
- Cited-by-URL depends on the model copying links (rule 6); measure it on
  real conversations before relying on it alone.
